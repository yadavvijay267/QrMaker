# 📷 QR Code Maker

> Create beautiful, custom QR codes in seconds — for links, text, WiFi, phone numbers and email. Style them with colors, shapes and your own logo, then download in high quality.

---

## ✨ About

**QR Code Maker** is a free, browser-based tool for making QR codes that don't look boring. Type your content and watch the QR update **live** as you design it — pick a color theme or your own colors, change the dot and corner shapes, drop your logo in the center, and download a print-ready image. Everything happens in your browser: nothing is uploaded to any server.

## 🚀 Features

- 🔗 **5 QR types** — URL/link, plain text, WiFi (SSID + password), phone number and email
- 👀 **Live preview** — the QR code rebuilds instantly with every change
- 🎨 **6 ready color themes** plus fully custom dot and background colors
- ✨ **Transparent background** option for overlays and print designs
- 🔷 **Shape styles** — multiple dot and corner designs, from classic squares to rounded and dotted
- 🖼️ **Logo in the center** — upload your own logo with an adjustable size slider
- ⬇️ **Download as PNG, JPG or SVG** — in 512, 1024 or 2048 px
- 📋 **Copy image** — paste the QR straight into any app
- 🔒 **Private by design** — all generation happens locally in the browser

## 🎮 How to Use

1. Pick a QR type — Link, Text, WiFi, Phone or Email.
2. Type your content; the preview updates live.
3. Choose a theme or your own colors, shapes and logo.
4. Download the QR as PNG, JPG or SVG — or copy the image.
